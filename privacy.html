Seattle Roleplay — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

1. Information We May Process

When account linking is used, the service may process your Discord user ID, Roblox user ID, Roblox username, and information needed to maintain the account association. The exact information depends on the features enabled.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to link accounts, confirm account associations, provide bot features, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot service problems.

3. Roblox Authorization

Roblox handles sign-in and authorization. The service should request only the permissions needed for its features. Never send your Roblox password or security codes to staff.

4. Storage and Security

Account-linking records and, if required by the implementation, authorization tokens may be stored on the service's hosting infrastructure. Access should be restricted to authorized operators. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

5. Sharing

Discord, Roblox, and infrastructure providers may process information as needed to deliver their respective services. Access by service operators should be limited to legitimate operational needs.

6. Retention and Deletion

Information should be retained only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law. To request unlinking or deletion of account-linking records, contact the Seattle Roleplay team through its official Discord server.

Deleting a record from Seattle Roleplay does not automatically delete information independently held by Discord or Roblox.

7. Age Requirements

Users must meet the applicable age requirements and follow the terms of Discord, Roblox, and relevant law.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the service or its data practices change. The current version will be published here.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the Seattle Roleplay team through its official Discord server.
